# Writing: Turn a Comment Into a Bounded Revision

Independent application worksheet; informed by Leey21 and the separately attributed Ding Xiaohan macro/micro Skills. Follow [the module map](contracts.md).

## Before Drafting

Identify the active section, paragraph purpose and exact comment. Read its dependencies in abstract, introduction, method, results and conclusion. Separate what the author wants to say, what the implementation supports and what was measured. For every paragraph, be able to state its question and answer in one sentence before changing it.

Choose the repair: explain a missing relation, reorder an existing explanation, define a term near first use, remove repetition, or narrow an unsupported assertion. Do not substitute a more elaborate synonym for an unresolved mechanism.

## Section-Specific Decisions

- Abstract: problem, supported mechanism, reported result, scope. Do not claim a guarantee contradicted in limitations. Preserve the actual comparison and denominator.
- Introduction: important problem -> precise gap -> why it is difficult -> final approach -> evidence. Map each challenge to a mechanism, not an implementation inventory. A first/only claim requires literature evidence, not a qualifier added for appearance.
- Method: explain the conceptual input, transformation and output before implementation rules. Distinguish inherited scaffold, new mechanism and heuristic. State what a selector selects and the observed criterion; a provenance field is not proof of semantic reasoning.
- Results: observation first, bounded interpretation second. A full-system comparison does not prove which component caused the gain. Preserve model, setting, count, metric and measured values.
- Discussion/conclusion: keep claims within the method and evidence already presented. Do not introduce a stronger generality or safety claim here.

## After Drafting

Read the original and candidate side by side. Preserve equations, citations, numbers and labels. Verify pronouns, transitions, terminology and neighboring references after edits. Leave a clear passage unchanged. Apply the author's concise style; decorative dashes, coined hyphen chains, code font and quotation marks need a real semantic purpose.

Keep the author's manuscript voice separate from an editorial audit. Do not turn an abstract into 'the task population remains unspecified', or narrate conflicts between the manuscript and its own figure labels in Results. Explain the supported observation plainly; place requests for missing records in the author-facing ledger. Keep genuine scientific limitations where they affect interpretation, but do not append a repeated catalog of unproven capabilities to every paragraph. If an efficiency headline cannot be interpreted without missing protocol, omit that headline from the abstract while retaining the reported measurements in Results and the open evidence request.

Synthetic example: a table comparing whole harnesses supports 'The harness increased the reported solve rate', not 'The router eliminated exploration'. Removing the causal attribution repairs prose; demonstrating the router's separate effect still requires evidence. Record both outcomes rather than marking the original scientific issue fully solved.
