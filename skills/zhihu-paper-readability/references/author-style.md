# Author Style and Changed-Passage Check

Project author's explicit preferences, separate from Ding Xiaohan's source principles. Apply to prose, not to valid LaTeX or established mathematical notation.

## Concise English

Prefer familiar verbs with an explicit subject and object. Explain what a mechanism does rather than stacking abstract nouns. Shorten repeated framing, filler and repeated results. Do not make all sentences short if that destroys an important condition or comparison.

Use a paragraph to answer one question; introduce a list only for a real enumeration. Preserve scientific specificity. Terms such as memory, selector and skill need operational meaning, not new promotional names.

Keep editorial diagnostics outside the manuscript. Avoid reviewer-voice sentences such as 'this manuscript does not specify...' in an abstract or 'the description and figure label do not establish...' in Results. Write the supported method and observation in the author's voice; keep missing-data requests in the revision ledger. Retain a necessary scientific limitation, but do not replace every contribution paragraph with denials of capabilities the paper no longer claims. Abstract, Introduction and Method must present the same two problems, not different lists of challenges.

## Typography With a Purpose

- Do not add decorative em/en dashes, repeated dash separators or coined chains such as evidence-guided-state-aware-learning when ordinary syntax is clearer.
- Do not mechanically remove established terms, negative signs, equation subtraction, citation page ranges or legitimate technical compounds.
- Use `\texttt{}` for a literal API/field/path whose exact spelling matters, not for emphasis on an ordinary concept. Preserve code typography inside a formal case definition when it identifies actual output values.
- Use quotation marks only for genuine quotation, a literal label or a necessary distinction. Avoid scare quotes around ordinary method concepts.
- Keep the manuscript language, citation keys, labels, equations and measured numbers unchanged unless an authorized scientific correction requires otherwise.

## Changed-Passage Verification

Compare before and after for subject, scope, comparison group, causal strength, uncertainty, units and all necessary conditions. Check the preceding and following sentences for broken references. If a proposed simplification changes any of those, restore the missing content or report why a substantive change is justified.

Do not globally delete every hyphen or every `\texttt` command. The goal is clear academic prose, not a token blacklist. Log which comment a real source change addresses and what remains unresolved.
