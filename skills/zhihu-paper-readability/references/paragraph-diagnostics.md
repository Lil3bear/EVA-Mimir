# Four-Axis Paragraph Diagnosis

Source principles: 丁霄汉, https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/639732057, from the user-supplied article. The decision table, revised examples and evidence safeguards below are independent adaptations. They do not endorse decorative citations or unsupported speculation.

| Axis | Inspect | Typical failure | Minimal repair | Not a repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Logical strength / 逻辑强度 | Actual relation between adjacent claims | A contrast compares architecture in one system with loss in another, leaving shared components ambiguous | Compare the same dimensions and state what stays fixed | Adding 'In contrast' to unrelated sentences |
| Defensibility / 防御弹性 | What a skeptical reader can challenge and what answers it | 'Prevents interference' is supported only by a file-edit boundary | Describe the enforced boundary; leave behavioral guarantees unestablished | Replacing 'proves' with 'suggests' without a basis |
| Comprehension delay / 迷惑时间 | Terms, pronouns, dependency order and first definitions | A new module name appears long before its function; 'which' has two possible antecedents | Explain its input/operation near first use; repeat the noun or split the sentence | Renaming the module with a more impressive noun |
| Information density / 信息密度 | Useful content per paragraph and relevance to its question | Historical setup or repeated table numbers hides the comparison | Remove irrelevant setup; state the comparison, magnitude and conditions directly | Removing denominator, unit or uncertainty to shorten the sentence |

## Logical Relations

For each connective ask: is the relation causal, contrastive, additive, sequential or an example? Are its operands explicit? Can it be removed without losing meaning? 'To this end' needs a stated goal. Parallel modules do not automatically have a temporal order. Do not imply a controlled comparison when model, architecture, data and objective all change together.

## Claim Strength

Distinguish measured observation from supported explanation and untested hypothesis. A whole-system gain does not isolate a component. A citation must be relevant, not merely abundant. Remove 'obviously', 'clearly superior' and similarly subjective emphasis when they replace the actual comparison. Neither polite hedging nor assertive wording is evidence.

## Definition and Reference Distance

Explain the substance of an unfamiliar component near its name, but keep the definition no longer than needed. Avoid unnecessary forward references while developing the main argument; include enough local context to preserve the reader's train of thought. Necessary equation, figure and source references remain intact.

## Dense Results Without Lost Meaning

Synthetic example: rates of 81.0% and 80.0% differ by **1.0 percentage point**, not automatically a 1.0% relative increase. Preserve the comparison population and any uncertainty. Place the main interpretation near the relevant table/caption and name the compared rows or conditions so a reader need not infer the intended comparison.

Return exact current quotes and minimal remedies. Leave already clear sentences unchanged. Do not generate a quota of language complaints or duplicate a scientific issue under four labels.
