# Narrative Design Worksheet

Source principle: 丁霄汉, https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/593195527, supplied by the author. The worksheet and synthetic examples are independent extensions, not quotations or claims about the source author's exact procedure.

## Recover the Final Work

Fill a compact map from the current manuscript and available artifacts:

| Field | Question | Failure signal | Defensible repair |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Problem | What concrete task or limitation motivates the paper? | A long field history before the task appears | Lead with the actual setting and consequence |
| Gap | What remains unresolved under the cited work's scope? | 'All prior work cannot...' without checked support | Name the exact dimension and preserve exceptions |
| Challenge | Why does that gap require a nontrivial choice? | A challenge is merely a module name or implementation step | State the competing requirements or failure condition |
| Mechanism | What input is transformed, how, and into what output? | A list of tools, parameters and safeguards replaces explanation | Explain the final conceptual operation before details |
| Ownership | What is inherited, adapted or newly proposed? | The only highlighted novelty is a small final adjustment to an unexplained author-built framework | Explicitly name the framework and adjustment, with attribution |
| Evidence | What result establishes which claim? | SOTA or a full-system gain stands in for every component's contribution | Match a result to its actual scope and keep missing controls open |

## From Chronology to Explanation

Do not make readers reconstruct the discovery path before seeing the final method. Present the complete final system, then explain why its components are needed. Retain scientifically meaningful failed approaches or negative results in analysis; restructuring is not permission to hide contrary evidence.

Synthetic weak outline: 'We tried A; A introduced B; after several attempts, trick C worked.'

Synthetic stronger outline: 'The method combines a framework and a retention criterion. The framework exposes candidate evidence; the criterion decides which revisions persist.' This organization is defensible only if both operations exist. It does not establish that rejected proposals automatically teach the next proposer or that admission proves generality.

## Challenge-to-Mechanism Test

Pair each challenge with a corresponding method operation and evidence. If Challenge 1 is inspectable state, name what records preserve that state and what recovery they do not provide. If Challenge 2 is update selection, explain the selection object, criterion and outcome rather than listing defensive rules. Describe narrow-skill consolidation as an aim or evidenced process, not an invented automatic orchestrator.

An important concept should be explained near its first appearance. The method opening should make the whole system understandable; later subsections elaborate it. Keep contribution statements about the final work, not the difficulty of doing the research.

## Decision

Return an outline repair when the supported content exists but its order obscures the message. Return an evidence request when a desired story requires an unimplemented mechanism or unreported result. Do not automatically ban 'based on', manufacture a new name for a combination, or force every paper into the same section template.
