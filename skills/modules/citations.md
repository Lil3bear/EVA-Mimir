# Citations: Syntax, Identity, Support

Independent application worksheet augmenting paper-pre-submission-review. These are three different checks; report their separate coverage.

## Local Source Check

Resolve every active citation key against the supplied Bib files. Locate duplicates, undefined keys, suspicious metadata and malformed field syntax. Inactive citations do not necessarily appear in the submitted PDF. Reformatting an unchanged page interval from a mixed dash to BibTeX `--` is a syntax repair, not external confirmation of those pages.

## Identity Check

Use an original paper, official proceedings/publisher record or authoritative supplied record to verify title, authors, year, version, venue and identifier. Do not combine metadata from different versions. A URL returning 200, an index match or a plausible DOI is not sufficient evidence of scientific support. Retain missing fields as unverified instead of filling them from memory.

## Support Check

Map the exact manuscript sentence to a relevant passage, result or protocol in the cited work. Distinguish absence of cross-task evaluation from proof that a method cannot transfer. For a novelty claim, specify the comparison dimension rather than saying all prior work lacks the capability. Preserve caveats and identify secondary transcriptions of baseline numbers.

## Repair Contract

Preserve keys and unrelated entries. Apply verified metadata corrections with provenance; qualify unsupported literature comparisons or report them pending. Record the number of entries checked for identity and the number of claims checked against full text. Do not call a local Bib parse a complete literature audit, and do not mark the paper novel merely because no match was found.
